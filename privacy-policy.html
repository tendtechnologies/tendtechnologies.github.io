Homesake — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Homesake helps you keep track of the appliances and maintenance in your home. This policy explains what it stores, where that goes, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read rather than to be legally impressive.

The short version

Homesake stores what you put into it. It works entirely on your phone unless you sign in, at which point your data also syncs to your own private area of our Firebase project so you can reach it from another device. Photos you scan are sent through Homesake's own server to Anthropic's Claude API to be read. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared for advertising, and there is no analytics or tracking in the app. If the app crashes, a crash report is sent so the bug can be fixed.

What is stored

  • What you enter — appliances and their brand, model, serial number, purchase date, price paid, location and notes; maintenance tasks and their history; improvement ideas; and your settings.
  • Photos you take or choose — data plates and receipts.
  • Documents you attach — warranties, manuals and receipts.
  • Account details, only if you sign in with Google — your name, email address, profile photo and a Google account identifier.
  • An anonymous identifier, created when the app first runs, used to count AI scans against a monthly allowance. It contains nothing personal and is not linked to you unless you sign in.
  • Subscription status, only if you subscribe to Homesake Pro or Homesake Landlord — whether a subscription is active, which tier and plan, and when the current period ends, plus a purchase token (a code from Google Play that identifies the subscription), kept on our server and linked to your account so the subscription can be checked. Homesake never sees your payment card or billing details.
  • Crash reports, only when the app crashes — the error, the screen it happened on, your phone model and Android version, and an installation identifier. They contain none of your records or photos.

Homesake does not collect your location, your contacts, your other apps, or anything you have not deliberately given it.

Where it goes

  • Your phone, always and first. Everything is written to the device before anything else happens, so the app works without a connection.
  • Google Firebase, if you sign in — Firestore for your records and Cloud Storage for your documents, both under an area only your account can read or write.
  • Anthropic's Claude API, when you use a scanning or lookup feature — the photo, or the brand and model you typed, goes to Homesake's server (Google Cloud Functions in our Firebase project), which sends it to Anthropic to be read and returns the result to your phone. For model lookups and manual searches, Claude may search the web using the brand and model. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models; see Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Google Play, if you subscribe to Homesake Pro or Landlord — the purchase, payment, free trial, renewal and cancellation are all handled by Google Play under Google's terms, not by Homesake. Homesake's server asks Google Play whether a subscription is active, using the purchase token, and stores the answer (status, tier, plan and end date) so paid features unlock on your devices. To cancel, use Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Firebase Crashlytics, if the app crashes — the crash report described above goes to Google so the bug can be found and fixed.
  • Manufacturer websites, when you ask Homesake to find a manual — it fetches the file directly from wherever it is published, so that site sees an ordinary web request.

Text recognition on data plates runs on your phone using Google ML Kit. That step sends nothing anywhere, and often means a scan completes without any photo leaving the device at all.

What is never done

  • No advertising, and no data shared with advertisers.
  • No selling of data to anyone, for any purpose.
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs.
  • No use of your content to train AI models.

Permissions

  • Internet — syncing, scanning and fetching manuals.
  • Notifications — maintenance reminders. Asked for when you first turn reminders on, not at first launch, and declining keeps everything else working.
  • Run at startup — reminders are rebuilt after a restart, because Android discards scheduled alarms when a phone reboots.

Photographs are taken through your phone's own camera app, so Homesake never holds camera permission itself.

How long it is kept

Your data stays until you delete it. Signing out deliberately leaves everything on your phone — signing out is not a request to lose your records, and treating it as one is how people lose things they cannot get back.

Deleting your data

  • A single item — delete the appliance, task or document in the app. It goes from the phone and from your synced copy.
  • Everything on the phone — uninstall, or clear the app's storage in Android settings.
  • Your account and everything synced to it — in the app, open Settings → Delete account and data and confirm. This immediately deletes your synced records, uploaded documents, scan history, subscription status and sign-in account from our servers, and clears the app's data from your phone. It cannot be undone. Without an account, the same row reads “Delete all data on this phone”. You can still ask by email instead: write to madhu.dev.apps@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and your records, documents and account are deleted within 30 days and confirmed by reply.

Children

Homesake is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for their use.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or where it goes, the updated version will appear here with a new date, and material changes will be noted in the app.
